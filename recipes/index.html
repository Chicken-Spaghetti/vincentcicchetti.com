Recipe Box (working title)

Your recipes, kept by your own AI. Coming soon.

This is a private test page for a recipe app that is still being built. Nothing here is live yet.

How it will work

Bring your own AI

Connect the AI you already use (Claude, ChatGPT, or Grok) to Recipe Box as an MCP connector.

Save & organize

Ask your AI to save, tag, and organize your personal recipes.

Scale & calculate

Scale servings up or down and get nutrition numbers calculated from USDA data.

See & edit

A web app and an Android app let you see and edit everything your AI did.

Plans

  • No free tier. Paid plans only; pricing to be announced.
  • 7-day free trial of the lowest tier.
  • A payment method is required up front to start the trial.
  • During the trial: up to 25 saved recipes and a daily limit on nutrition lookups.
