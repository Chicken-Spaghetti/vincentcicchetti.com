Weeknight Garlic Pasta

Prep 5 min · Cook 20 min · Saved by your AI
4 servings

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Save 1 cup of pasta water.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil and garlic over medium-low heat until golden, about 5 minutes. Add pepper flakes.
  3. Toss the drained pasta in the pan with a splash of pasta water.
  4. Stir in Parmesan and parsley. Loosen with more pasta water and serve.
