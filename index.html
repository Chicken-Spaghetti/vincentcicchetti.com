About

I have spent most of my working life close to people and close to problems that have to be solved with both hands. That includes running a massage practice, directing a spa, turning wrenches at Pep Boys, remodeling houses with a small team, serving as a full-time caregiver under Florida’s NICA program, and completing a cybersecurity professional boot camp at the University of Central Florida.

Most recently I returned to life, health, and annuity work as an independent agent affiliated with Brokers Alliance. The through-line is the same: listen carefully, do the work cleanly, keep learning.
