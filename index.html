About

Most of the work I have done starts the same way: someone has a problem, I look at it, and I stay with it until it is done. That has meant remodeled houses, shop work at Pep Boys, a massage practice and a few years running a spa floor, full-time care under Florida’s NICA program, and a lot of smaller repair jobs at home and for other people.

Hands-on work

The two remodels below are the biggest examples. Around them I have painted, laid floors, hung fixtures, repaired appliances, worked on cars, and fixed keyboards and other electronics — including instruments at church. I also build portable speakers when I get a chance.

New Port Richey remodel — Stell Drive

Whole-house renovation with a small team. Pasco County treated the work as changing the house’s effective age.

  • Replaced a gable roof with a hip roof and redid the stucco
  • Full rewire and a new breaker panel
  • Raised the foundation in the primary bedroom
  • All new drywall, paint, and flooring
  • Kitchen and bath cabinets, counters, and fixtures
  • Replumbed the kitchen and laundry; installed an Eemax tankless water heater
  • Closet doors and shelving; aluminum windows replaced with vinyl
  • Backyard sump pump to keep the laundry room dry in hurricane season

Leesburg remodel — Fairway Circle · 2020–2021

A tighter scope than Stell Drive: paint, floors, and countertops, then a real addition. We enclosed the back porch on a raised wood platform foundation, and built a family room and bathroom — the rooms with the sliding barn doors.
