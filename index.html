About

I have been working with my hands for a long time. Some of that work had a shop name on the paycheck. A lot of it did not. The habit is the same either way: look at the problem, figure out what it needs, and finish clean.

That has included remodeling houses with a small team, turning wrenches at Pep Boys in Leesburg, running a massage practice, and co-owning Therapy Touch for four years. Since 2021 I have also been a full-time family caregiver under Florida’s NICA program. In between I keep taking the jobs that show up — paint, floors, a fixture, an appliance, a car that will not start, a church keyboard that lost its sound.

Hands-on work

The two houses below are the easiest way to show the scale of it. Stell Drive was a gut. Fairway was smaller, then we closed in the porch and added rooms. I still do the quieter repair work around those projects. The photos are ones I took for the listing agent.

New Port Richey remodel — Stell Drive · 2014–2016

Whole-house renovation in Pasco County. I was the owner-builder and led a small team. Pasco County treated the work as changing the house’s effective age.

  • Drew the electrical and plumbing rough-in plans, approved by an engineer and Pasco County
  • Licensed subcontractors did the rough-in, including the panel work
  • Did my own inspections at each stage and scheduled the county inspections
  • Replaced a gable roof with a hip roof and redid the stucco
  • Raised the foundation in the primary bedroom
  • All the finish work: drywall, paint, flooring, kitchen and bath cabinets, counters, and fixtures
  • Finish plumbing in the kitchen and laundry; installed an Eemax tankless water heater after the rough-in
  • Closet doors and shelving; aluminum windows replaced with vinyl
  • Backyard sump pump to keep the laundry room dry in hurricane season

Leesburg remodel — Fairway Circle · 2020–2021

Fairway was simpler. Paint, floors, countertops. Then we enclosed the back porch on a raised wood platform — concrete like Stell would have cost too much — and built a family room and a bathroom. Those are the rooms with the sliding barn doors.
