About

Most of my working life has been the same loop: listen, look at the problem, fix what is broken, and clean up after myself. That loop has covered whole-house remodels, shop service at Pep Boys, a massage practice and spa floor, full-time skilled family care under Florida’s NICA program, and general repair on homes and vehicles.

I am looking for W-2 or other base-pay work within about 15 miles of Harmony, Florida, or remote work that pays a printed base. I am not looking for commission-only sales.
